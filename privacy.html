Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy covers Bounce Rumble Uploader ("the app"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Bounce Rumble YouTube channel. The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app also uses the TikTok Content Posting API to post the same videos to the Bounce Rumble TikTok account. TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Who uses the app

Only the channel owner. The app is not offered to the public and does not sign in other users.

What data the app accesses

  • OAuth tokens for the channel owner's Google account, used to call the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.
  • Channel data of the Bounce Rumble channel: video metadata (titles, descriptions, publish times), channel branding settings.
  • Aggregated analytics of the Bounce Rumble channel's own videos: views, watch time, average view duration and audience retention. These are channel-level and video-level totals from YouTube Analytics. They contain no personal data about individual viewers.
  • TikTok: OAuth tokens for the owner's TikTok account, the account's basic profile (display name, username), the privacy options TikTok offers for new posts, and the processing status of the app's own uploads.

The app does not collect data about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube users.

How the data is used

  • Uploading the channel's own videos and setting their titles, descriptions and publish times.
  • Updating the channel's own branding (banner, description, keywords).
  • Comparing how the channel's videos perform so the next videos can be planned.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth credentials are kept as secrets in the owner's private configuration and private cloud environment. They are never published or shared.
  • Aggregated analytics may be stored in the owner's private code repository as small summary files to plan future videos. They are kept no longer than 30 days unless refreshed from the API.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties, and none is used for advertising.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (remove "Bounce Rumble Uploader"), and for TikTok under Settings → Security → Apps and services in the TikTok app. After revocation the app can no longer reach the account, and stored analytics summaries are deleted within 7 days on request.
